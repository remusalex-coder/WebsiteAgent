# 03 — PREMIUM STACK (€5–€20+ justified)

> PREMIUM = top-tier quality, brand-safe licensing, highest control. Used ONLY when
> business evidence (brand tier, conversion data, client requirement) justifies the spend.
> Typically 1–3 hero assets, never the whole site.

## When to escalate MEDIUM → PREMIUM
- Luxury/automotive/fashion brand where hero video quality is the product differentiator.
- Client requires enterprise/commercial-safe licensing with SLA (Adobe Firefly, enterprise).
- Voice-over is core (narrated brand film) → ElevenLabs Pro low-latency 44.1kHz.
- Multiple product 3D configurators at scale → Meshy Studio/Enterprise.
- Cinematic director-controlled shots → Higgsfield Ultra / Veo 3 / Runway Max.
